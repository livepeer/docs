{/* ## Purpose & Aims
Livepeer documentation exists as a canonical source of truth for all information related to Livepeer's technology, ecosystem, and community.
It serves as a critical resource for educating, onboarding, and empowering our diverse range of stakeholders, including developers, delegators, gateway operators, orchestrators, and community members.

Orient → Activate → Enable.

## Docs Philosophy

1. Documentation is not static. It is infrastructure & must be built, maintained, and governed as such.
2. AI is the new search, and discoverable products are AI-first
3. Agents are the new docs consumers and users and information should be structured accordingly

Journey-based, persona-first, AI-first & future-proofed documentation that is built as infrastructure, not a one-time project.

            <Accordion title="New Orchestrator">

                1. Introduction to Livepeer and its value proposition
            </Accordion>
            <Accordion title="Experienced Orchestrator">

            </Accordion>
        </AccordionGroup>
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Delegator">
        Delegators are individuals who delegate their tokens to orchestrators in the Livepeer network. They need documentation that helps them understand the delegation process, choose orchestrators, and manage their stakes effectively.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Solution Integrator">
        Solution Integrators are individuals or organizations that integrate Livepeer's technology into their own solutions. They need documentation that helps them understand how to leverage Livepeer's offerings to enhance their products and services.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Developer">
        Developers are building with Livepeer's APIs, SDKs, and tools. They are looking for clear, concise documentation that helps them understand how to use Livepeer's offerings to build their applications.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Gateway Operator">
        Gateway Operators are running Livepeer's software to contribute to the network and earn rewards. They need documentation that helps them set up, manage, and optimize their nodes effectively.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Community">
        Community members are individuals who are interested in learning about Livepeer, staying updated on its developments, and engaging with other members. They need documentation that provides an accessible introduction to Livepeer's technology, use cases, and impact.

</AccordionGroup>
