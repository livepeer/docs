Running an orchestrator means operating a **go-livepeer** node that provides GPU compute (video transcoding and/or AI inference) to the Livepeer network. You earn from protocol rewards (LPT) and job fees (ETH).

## Setup checklist

1. **Hardware** - [Hardware requirements](./rcs-requirements)
2. **Install** - [Install go-livepeer](./rs-install)
3. **Connect** - [Connect to Arbitrum](../unclassified/connect-to-arbitrum)
4. **Configure** - [Configure your node](./r-configure)
5. **Run and activate** - [Activate on the network](../unclassified/rcs-connect-activate-publish)
6. **Monitor** - [Orchestrator stats and monitoring](./r-monitor)
