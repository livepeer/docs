Livepeer enables node operators to transcode video on GPUs while concurrently
mining cryptocurrencies and performing other CUDA operations such as machine
learning. As there is a very wide range of GPU hardware out there, this document
aims to crowdsource a list of specific GPU models that have been tested and
verified to work on Livepeer. If you've tested an additional model, please
submit an update to this document.

## Overview

- Livepeer supports transcoding on NVIDIA GPUs with NVENC/NVDEC. Any GPU
  [listed here](https://developer.nvidia.com/video-encode-and-decode-gpu-support-matrix-new)
  with those chips should theoretically work. Note that different models may be
  subject to different session limits that restrict the amount of video that can
  be transcoded.
- See
  [this document which lists tested driver versions](https://github.com/livepeer/go-livepeer/blob/master/doc/gpu.md)
  on the NVIDIA cards.

## Transcoding GPUs

HEVC (H.265) support is increasingly important on the Livepeer network. All GPUs based on Maxwell architecture (GTX 950) and newer support HEVC encode and decode via NVENC/NVDEC. Refer to [NVIDIA's encode/decode support matrix](https://developer.nvidia.com/video-encode-and-decode-gpu-support-matrix-new) for the full list.

## GPUs for AI Workloads

The following GPUs are recommended for running AI inference workloads on Livepeer. Higher VRAM capacity allows loading larger models, while newer architectures (Ampere, Ada Lovelace, Hopper) offer improved throughput for AI tasks.

| GPU Model               | VRAM   | Architecture   | Notes                                                         |
| ----------------------- | ------ | -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3090 | 24 GB  | Ampere         | High VRAM consumer option; good for mid-size AI models        |
| NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4080 | 16 GB  | Ada Lovelace   | Excellent price-to-performance for AI inference               |
| NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4090 | 24 GB  | Ada Lovelace   | Best consumer GPU for AI inference; high throughput           |
| NVIDIA RTX 4000 Ada SFF | 20 GB  | Ada Lovelace   | Compact professional card; good for space-constrained servers |
| NVIDIA RTX 6000 Ada     | 48 GB  | Ada Lovelace   | Professional card; supports very large AI models              |
| NVIDIA A40              | 48 GB  | Ampere         | Data center card; high VRAM for large model inference         |
| NVIDIA A100             | 80 GB  | Ampere         | Top-tier data center GPU; excellent AI training and inference |
| NVIDIA L40S             | 48 GB  | Ada Lovelace   | Data center inference GPU; strong throughput for AI workloads |
| NVIDIA H100             | 80 GB  | Hopper         | Highest performance data center GPU for AI workloads          |
