The Developers tab assumes you already know what Livepeer is in broad strokes. The concept pages here close the gap between that broad picture and the build pages by giving you the layer your application sits at, the surfaces that connect those layers, and the repositories behind each one.

Four pages cover the model. Each one stands alone and links to the others where the model overlaps.
