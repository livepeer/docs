Extending the protocol means touching go-livepeer internals, smart contracts on Arbitrum, or the core off-chain mechanisms behind probabilistic micropayments and orchestrator selection. Two tabs cover this work: About documents the protocol as it stands, and Community covers contribution, governance, and treasury participation.
