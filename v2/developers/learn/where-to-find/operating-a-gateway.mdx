A developer running a self-hosted gateway to cut cost or gain control is doing two distinct things: shipping their application, and operating gateway infrastructure. The application side stays in the Developers tab. The infrastructure side, including production hardening, monitoring, scaling, and payment configuration, lives in the Gateways tab.
