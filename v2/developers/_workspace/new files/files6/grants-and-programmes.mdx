The Network Engineering SPE is the primary fast-track funding mechanism for developer contributions in the $2k–$20k range. It operates two tracks: RFPs (work scoped and published by the Foundation) and retroactive grants (payment for work already completed).

<CustomDivider middleText="Applying for an RFP" />

## Applying to an Open RFP

RFPs are published on the Livepeer forum and roadmap when the Foundation has scoped and validated a piece of work.

<StyledSteps iconColor="#2d9a67" titleColor="var(--accent)">
  <StyledStep title="Find open RFPs" icon="search">
    Check the Livepeer Forum at `forum.livepeer.org` (Treasury category) and the roadmap at `roadmap.livepeer.org` for published RFPs. New RFPs are announced in the `#builders` channel in the Livepeer Discord.

    Each RFP specifies the scope, deliverables, timeline, and payment range.
  </StyledStep>

  <StyledStep title="Submit an application" icon="file">
    Reply to the forum thread for the RFP you are applying to. Include:

    - Your background and relevant work
    - Your approach to the deliverables
    - Estimated timeline
    - Any open questions about scope

    Applications are reviewed by the Foundation. Assignment is made within 7 days of the RFP closing date.
  </StyledStep>

  <StyledStep title="Agree on scope and start" icon="handshake">
    Once assigned, the Foundation issues a brief scope agreement. Payment is split in three tranches: 25% on signing, 50% on delivery and verification by the review team, 25% on impact assessment at the end of the SPE pilot.
  </StyledStep>

  <StyledStep title="Deliver and get paid" icon="check">
    Complete the work to the agreed deliverables. The review team verifies completion. The 50% delivery payment is released on verification. The final 25% is paid at the end of the pilot period based on impact.
  </StyledStep>
</StyledSteps>

<CustomDivider middleText="Suggesting an RFP" />

## Suggesting a New RFP

If you have an idea for work that would benefit the network but no RFP exists for it, post a Roadmap Suggestion on the forum. The community discussion process converts validated suggestions into funded RFPs.

<StyledSteps iconColor="#2d9a67" titleColor="var(--accent)">
  <StyledStep title="Post a Roadmap Suggestion" icon="lightbulb">
    Post on the Livepeer Forum under the Treasury category. Include the problem being solved, proposed deliverables, estimated scope, and why the work benefits the network. Tag it as a Roadmap Suggestion.
  </StyledStep>

  <StyledStep title="Community validation" icon="users">
    The Foundation facilitates discussion in Discord. Strong community validation moves the suggestion to the scoping stage. Suggestions that don't achieve consensus are archived.
  </StyledStep>

  <StyledStep title="Foundation publishes RFP" icon="publish">
    Once validated, the Foundation publishes a finalised RFP with defined deliverables. At that point the standard RFP application process opens.
  </StyledStep>
</StyledSteps>

<CustomDivider middleText="Retroactive Grants" />

## Retroactive Grants

Retroactive grants fund work that has already been completed and has demonstrably benefited the Livepeer network or developer ecosystem. No prior approval is required.

To submit a retroactive grant application:

1. Post on the Livepeer Forum under the Treasury category
2. Describe the work completed, the evidence of impact, and the funding amount requested
3. Include links to the shipped work (GitHub PRs, deployed tools, published content)
4. The Foundation reviews and decides on payment within the SPE's pilot timeframe

Retroactive grants are well-suited for open-source contributors who shipped improvements to go-livepeer, documentation, tooling, or ecosystem infrastructure without prior funding.

<CustomDivider middleText="Larger SPE Proposals" />

## Proposing an SPE

For larger programmes ($50k+) requiring multi-month sustained work, the full SPE process applies:

1. Post a pre-proposal on the forum (Treasury category) with abstract, budget, timeline, team, and deliverables
2. Gather community feedback and revise
3. Submit the on-chain governance proposal
4. LPT holders vote; proposals passing the approval threshold receive treasury funding

SPE proposals require significant community trust-building before they receive votes. The forum discussion period is important: well-validated proposals with clear deliverables and known teams pass reliably.

<Note>
  All forum discussions, proposal decisions, and grant disbursements are public. Decisions include written rationale from the Foundation. The full SPE process documentation is maintained in the Livepeer Foundation Notion workspace.
</Note>
