## Network Engineering SPE

The Network Engineering SPE (proposed April 2026 by Rich O'Grady and Rick Starr, Livepeer Foundation) is a delegated pool of capital targeting the $2k–$20k engineering work that the full SPE process is too slow to fund. It covers three priority areas:

**Priority 1: Developer Portal (The 5-Minute API).** Engineering that takes a developer from documentation to first inference call in under five minutes from any MCP-compatible tool. Includes Python SDK work, BYOC container tooling, payment and auth infrastructure, agentic harness tooling.

**Priority 2: Explorer (Participation and Observability).** Work that makes the Explorer a better participation portal. Includes staking interfaces, governance features, network observability dashboards.

**Priority 3: Orchestrator Tooling and Infrastructure.** Containerisation, orchestration tooling, go-livepeer contributions, infrastructure that improves network reliability.

RFPs are published on the Livepeer forum and roadmap. Community members can also suggest new RFPs by posting on the forum. Follow `#builders` in the Livepeer Discord for updates.

**RFP process:** Published RFP → contributor application → assignment within 7 days → 25% on signing, 50% on delivery, 25% on impact at end of pilot.

## SPE Funding

SPEs (Special Purpose Entities) are working groups funded by the on-chain community treasury to execute on specific programmes over a defined period. They are accountable to the community through regular reporting.

Recent funded SPEs relevant to developers:

- **Agent SPE** (Phase 1: January 2025, 6,440 LPT; Phase 2: March 2025, 30,000 LPT): Eliza plugin integrations, VTuber streaming agents
- **Streamplace** (April 2025, 100,000 LPT): Decentralised livestreaming SDKs and social protocol integrations
- **Transformation SPE** (September 2025, 44,500 LPT): Capital, Builders, and Development workstreams

To propose an SPE, post a pre-proposal on the Livepeer forum under the Treasury category, gather community feedback, and submit the on-chain proposal.
