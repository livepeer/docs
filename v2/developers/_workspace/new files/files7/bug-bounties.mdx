Livepeer runs a bug bounty programme on Immunefi at `immunefi.com/bug-bounty/livepeer/`. The programme covers smart contract vulnerabilities in the Livepeer protocol contracts deployed on Arbitrum One and Ethereum.

<CustomDivider middleText="Programme Details" />

## Scope and Rewards

**Scope:** Smart contracts only. Websites, applications, and off-chain infrastructure are not in scope.

**Reward currency:** USDC on Ethereum, denominated in USD. Payouts are handled by the Livepeer team directly.

**Critical vulnerability cap:** Rewards for critical vulnerabilities are capped at 10% of the economic damage, with primary focus on possible loss of funds for Orchestrators, Delegators, and Broadcasters.

**High vulnerability rewards:** Depend on the amount of unclaimed yield at risk and how long funds could be frozen.

**KYC:** Required for all reporters claiming a reward. Visual proof of identity is collected by the project team.

**Proof of concept:** Required for all severity levels.

<CustomDivider middleText="Primary Impacts" />

## In-Scope Impacts

The programme focuses on preventing:

- Direct theft of any user funds, whether at-rest or in-motion, other than unclaimed yield
- Permanent freezing of funds
- Unexpected calls to functions that should only be called by authorised addresses (e.g. Governor)

<CustomDivider middleText="Submitting a Report" />

## Submission

Submit all reports through the Immunefi platform at `immunefi.com/bug-bounty/livepeer/`. Do not report security vulnerabilities in public GitHub issues. The Immunefi submission process handles triage, severity assessment, and payout coordination.

For questions about programme scope before submitting, reach out in the `#security` channel in the Livepeer Discord or contact the team via the Immunefi platform.

<Note>
  The complete programme terms, reward schedule, scope details, and known excluded vulnerabilities are maintained on Immunefi and may be updated by the Livepeer team. The Immunefi page is the authoritative source.
</Note>
