  <Tip>BYOC (Bring Your Own Container) lets you run any AI model on the Livepeer network. Package your model in Docker, implement the FrameProcessor interface, and register it as a network capability.</Tip>

<CustomDivider />

The Livepeer network supports nine native AI pipelines (text-to-image, audio-to-text, LLM, etc.) out of the box. For models that do not fit a native pipeline, BYOC lets you deploy arbitrary Docker containers as network capabilities.

A BYOC container receives live video frames over the trickle protocol, processes them through your model, and publishes the results back. From the network's perspective, your container looks identical to a native pipeline: gateways discover it through the AI Service Registry, route jobs to it, and pay for it through the standard payment mechanism.

BYOC reached production-grade in Phase 4 (January 2026). Embody SPE (now MuxionLabs) and Streamplace run production BYOC workloads on the network.
