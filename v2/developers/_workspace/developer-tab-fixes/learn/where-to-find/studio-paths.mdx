Livepeer Studio is the longest-running product surface on the network. Studio handles ingest, transcoding, playback, VOD, AI inference, webhooks, and analytics behind a hosted API and dashboard. The rest of this tab covers network-direct paths that bypass Studio, but Studio remains the right answer for many use cases.

## Network-Direct Decision Criteria

Three criteria justify moving network-direct.

| Criterion | What it means | Where to go |
|---|---|---|
| Cost at scale | Per-minute or per-call billing exceeds the cost of running your own gateway and absorbing variance | <LinkArrow href="/v2/gateways/gateways-portal" label="Gateways tab" newline={false} /> |
| Control and sovereignty | Custom orchestrator selection, pricing logic, or signer custody requirements | <LinkArrow href="/v2/gateways/gateways-portal" label="Gateways tab" newline={false} /> |
| AI-only workload | Inference is the only need; video ingest and managed playback are unused | <LinkArrow href="/v2/gateways/gateways-portal" label="Gateways tab" newline={false} /> |

Each criterion stands on its own. A workload that meets any one is a candidate for network-direct.

Migration mechanics, account closure, and data export are documented in the Studio side of the Solutions tab.
