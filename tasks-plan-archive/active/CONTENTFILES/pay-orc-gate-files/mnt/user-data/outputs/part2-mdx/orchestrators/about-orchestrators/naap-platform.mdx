# NaaP Platform for Orchestrators

<Callout type="info">
**Status: In development.** NaaP (Network as a Platform) is targeting an MVP launch in 2026. Orchestrators do not need to take any action now — NaaP reads your existing on-chain capability announcements automatically.
</Callout>

The **NaaP Platform** (Network as a Platform) is the Livepeer Foundation's coordinated access layer for the network. For orchestrators (service providers), NaaP functions as an observability and discovery layer — surfacing performance data to the ecosystem and enabling capacity commitments that bring structured demand.

## What NaaP Means for Orchestrators

You are **Persona 3 (P3)** in the NaaP model: a service provider contributing GPU compute to the network. NaaP's value to you is:

### Network Performance Dashboard

NaaP aggregates real-time performance metrics across the orchestrator network and surfaces them in a public dashboard:

- Per-orchestrator latency, throughput, and reliability scores
- Pipeline-level availability and error rates
- Comparative rankings across the active orchestrator set

This gives gateway operators and delegators objective data for routing and staking decisions. Strong performance on NaaP metrics should translate to more delegated stake and higher job volume over time.

### Discovery and Visibility

NaaP reads your existing on-chain registrations — your `ServiceRegistry` entry and `AIServiceRegistry` capabilities — and surfaces them through the platform. No additional registration steps are required.

As NaaP develops, it may offer enhanced visibility features: tagging specialised capabilities, geographic presence, hardware class, and average response times. These signals help gateways make better routing decisions and help developers find orchestrators suited to their specific workload.

### Capacity Planner

NaaP includes a **capacity planner** enabling orchestrators to make soft-commitments to specific pipeline capacity requests:

- View upcoming demand projections for AI pipelines
- Indicate how many concurrent jobs you can serve for a given model
- Receive matched demand through NaaP-aware gateways and service providers

Soft-commitments are not binding — they are signals that help coordinate supply and demand across the network without requiring centralised allocation.

### Plugin System

NaaP's plugin marketplace enables orchestrators to expose additional capabilities beyond the base pipeline set:

- Custom live-video-to-video pipelines can be registered as plugins
- Orchestrators supporting specialised models (e.g. domain-specific LLMs, custom ComfyUI workflows) gain targeted discovery
- Demand from service providers is routed to orchestrators via plugin subscriptions

## No Immediate Action Required

NaaP reads your existing orchestrator registrations automatically. To be visible on NaaP when it launches:

1. Be an [active orchestrator](/v2/orchestrators/quickstart/realtime-ai) with staked LPT in the top-100 active set.
2. Register your AI capabilities via the `AIServiceRegistry` contract — see the [Realtime AI quickstart](/v2/orchestrators/quickstart/realtime-ai) for instructions.
3. Maintain competitive performance metrics (low latency, high availability) — these will be tracked by NaaP.
